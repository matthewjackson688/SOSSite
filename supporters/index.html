Get Involved and Support Us

Have these stories resonated with you? #SOS is about understanding and sharing the direct experiences of people with disabilities in Africa and Wales. There are several ways you can get involved and support our work.

  1. Join the #SOS WhatsApp chat. If you are a disabled person, or part of an organisation run by disabled people, we invite you to join the #SOS WhatsApp chat. Click here
  2. Follow us on social media. Whoever you are, you can follow us on our current social media platforms, Facebook and Instagram. Follow and like our pages to keep up to date with #SOS and our latest stories and activities.
  3. Share your organisation with our community. If you are an organisation, we invite you to have your logo featured on our webpage. Click here
  4. Help make services more disability inclusive. Get in touch to explore how your organisation, company, or service could help remove barriers and become more disability inclusive. Click here
  5. Support our work. If you would like to support #SOS through donations of funds, materials, or other in-kind support, click here
